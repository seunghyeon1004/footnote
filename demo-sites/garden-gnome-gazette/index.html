Why your indoor compost smells (and the 3-minute fix)

A smelly bin is a bin telling you something.

Odor is almost always too much moisture, too little air, or too many greens. Mix in dry browns like shredded paper or cardboard to soak up liquid, freeze scraps until you are ready to add them, and keep a tight lid on anything anaerobic.
