Bokashi in 40 square feet: what 14 days really looks like

I fermented every food scrap from a one-bedroom flat for a year. Here is the timeline that finally made it boring, in the good sense.

The core numbers

A bokashi bucket does not compost. It ferments scraps with inoculated bran in an airtight container. At a steady 20 to 25 degrees Celsius a full batch is pre-compost after about 14 days. Below 15 degrees it stalls, and above 30 it can turn putrid.

Layer scraps and a spoonful of bran, press out the air, and close the lid. Drain the liquid every two days and dilute it 1:100 for houseplants.

After fermenting

The result is pickled, not finished. Bury it in soil or a large planter for another two weeks, or add it to a worm bin as a small percentage of the feed. Expect a sweet, vinegary smell; rot means air got in.
