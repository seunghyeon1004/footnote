Open Compost Toolkit

open-compost-toolkit

MIT-licensed firmware and schematics for a moisture sensor that sits in any compost bin.

README

Indoor bins fail in two ways: too dry (nothing happens) or too wet (smell). The sweet spot is 50 to 60 percent moisture by weight. The sensor reports a reading every 10 minutes and flashes red outside that range.
