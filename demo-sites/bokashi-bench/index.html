Bokashi vs worm bin: a four-week bench test

Two kitchens, identical scraps, one sniff panel.

The sealed bokashi bucket scored lowest for odor in every week. The worm bin took less hands-on time once established, and produced finished castings rather than pre-compost. Space was a tie: both fit under a standard sink.
