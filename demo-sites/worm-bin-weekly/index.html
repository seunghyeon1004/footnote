A worm bin under the sink: feeding limits that actually hold

Red wigglers are the quietest roommates you will ever have, if you respect the numbers.

Capacity

One pound of red wigglers (Eisenia fetida) processes about half a pound of food scraps per day. A single person generates roughly that much, so a pound of worms in a 10-gallon bin is a good starting point.

What to avoid

Keep citrus, onion, garlic, meat, dairy and oily food out. Bury scraps under bedding, and feed only when the previous meal is mostly gone. Overfeeding is the cause of nearly every smell and fruit-fly outbreak.
