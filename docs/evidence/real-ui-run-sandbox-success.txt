## Question / answer
How can I compost food scraps in a small apartment?

Two methods suit apartments: a sealed bokashi bucket or an under-sink worm bin.

**Choosing:** In a four-week test, the sealed bokashi bucket had the lowest odor every week. The worm bin needed less hands-on time once established and produced finished castings. Both fit under a standard sink. [1]

**Worm bin basics:** One pound of red wigglers handles about half a pound of scraps a day, roughly one person's output, so start with a pound in a 10-gallon bin. Avoid citrus, onion, garlic, meat, dairy and oily food. Bury scraps under bedding and feed only when the last meal is mostly gone. Overfeeding causes nearly every smell and fruit-fly problem. [2]

**Controlling smell:** Odor usually comes from too much moisture, too little air, or too many greens. Add dry browns such as shredded paper or cardboard, freeze scraps until you're ready to add them, and keep anaerobic bins tightly lidded. [3]

**Everyday habits:** Chop scraps smaller than a thumbnail so they break down faster. Keep a small counter container and empty it daily so scraps don't sit and smell. [4]
