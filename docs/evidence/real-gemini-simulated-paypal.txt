ANSWER:
Composting food scraps in a small apartment is best done with space-saving indoor methods like vermicomposting or bokashi fermentation:

* **Vermicomposting (Worm Bins):** A 10-gallon bin under the sink housing one pound of red wigglers (*Eisenia fetida*) can process about half a pound of scraps per day [1]. To avoid odors and fruit flies, bury scraps under bedding and exclude citrus, onions, garlic, meat, dairy, and oily foods [1].
* **Bokashi Fermentation:** Bokashi ferments scraps anaerobically in a sealed bucket using inoculated bran [2]. It fits under standard sinks and yields low odor when kept airtight [3]. Layer scraps with bran, press out air, and drain liquid every two days (diluting 1:100 for houseplants) [2]. After about 14 days, bury the pickled pre-compost in soil or large planters [2].

**Key Tips for Success:**
* Chop scraps smaller than thumbnail size to increase surface area for faster processing [4].
* Maintain bin moisture around 50–60%; excessive moisture causes foul odors, while dry conditions stall decomposition [5].
